SubDocs Privacy Policy

Applies to the SubDocs iOS app (bundle com.amico.subdoc) and the SubDocs web app at subdoc.amico.solutions.
Effective date: November 12, 2026

1. Who we are

SubDocs is an internal construction-compliance tool operated by AMICO (the AMICO group of companies, “AMICO”, “we”, “us”). It is used by AMICO staff and by the subcontractor companies AMICO works with to manage project compliance documents, worker certificates, safety records and related communications. The iOS app is published on the App Store under the A-Linx Building Technologies developer account.

SubDocs is not a consumer service. Accounts are created by AMICO for its staff and for subcontractor companies; members of the public cannot sign up.

2. Information we collect

AMICO staff accounts. Name, work email address, optional username and profile image, role and permissions, business-unit memberships, account status, sign-in and session records. Staff sign in with their AMICO Microsoft (Microsoft Entra ID) account; Microsoft returns the staff member’s name and email to SubDocs. To keep staff signed in on iOS, the server may store an encrypted Microsoft refresh token.

Subcontractor company accounts. Company name, contact person, email address and phone number, business and WSIB numbers, project assignments, the company login username and a hashed (not readable) password, notification settings, and sign-in and security records.

Subcontractor workers / employees. Name, optional email address and phone number, company, role and status, required certificate types, certificate files, expiry dates and review status.

Compliance documents and files. Documents uploaded by subcontractors or staff (for example insurance certificates, WSIB clearances, CVOR abstracts, permits and other project compliance documents), along with file names, dates, expiry dates and review decisions.

Safety and orientation records. Daily Task Hazard Analysis (THA) answers, work dates and projects, attendees, acknowledgements, drawn signatures, uploaded photos and PDFs, and site-orientation records, which can include an emergency contact name and phone number and training information.

Ratings, notes and messages. Performance ratings and the names of the staff who gave them, internal staff notes about subcontractors, messages, and a record of emails sent through SubDocs (recipients, subject, content and delivery status).

Technical information. IP address and browser/device user agent in sign-in and audit records; for the iOS app, a push-notification token, device identifier, platform, app version and last-seen time; and server and audit logs.

3. Device permissions on iOS

  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to take or select a photo or file to upload (for example a document or THA photo). You can decline; other features still work.
  • Files: only when you choose a document to upload.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional): used only to unlock your saved SubDocs sign-in on your own device. Biometric matching is performed by iOS; SubDocs never receives or stores your face or fingerprint data.
  • Notifications (optional): used to send compliance and project notifications. You can turn them off in iOS Settings or in the app.
  • Motion: the launch animation reads device motion on the device only; it is not stored or sent to us.

The app does not collect your location, does not access your contacts, contains no advertising, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

4. How we use information

  • To authenticate users and keep accounts secure.
  • To collect, review and track subcontractor compliance documents, certificates and expiries for AMICO projects.
  • To record daily THA and site-orientation safety information.
  • To let AMICO staff rate and keep notes on subcontractor performance.
  • To send account, compliance-reminder and project emails and push notifications.
  • To maintain audit records, troubleshoot problems and protect the service.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

5. Who we share information with

Within AMICO, information is visible to authorized staff according to their role and business unit. A subcontractor company can see its own company, workers, documents and assignments; it cannot see other companies’ data or AMICO’s internal notes and ratings.

We use service providers that process data on our behalf to run SubDocs:

  • Replit – application hosting, logs and the managed PostgreSQL database.
  • Microsoft (Entra ID, Microsoft Graph and SharePoint) – staff sign-in and storage of uploaded documents, certificates and safety files.
  • DigitalOcean Spaces and Replit App Storage (Google Cloud Storage) – file storage and upload staging.
  • SendGrid (Twilio) – sending emails.
  • Expo and Apple Push Notification service – delivering iOS push notifications.
  • On the web app only: OpenStreetMap map tiles and Google Fonts, which receive standard network information (such as IP address) when your browser loads them.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect people’s safety, or in connection with a corporate reorganization of AMICO.

6. Where information is stored

Our service providers may store and process information in Canada, the United States or other countries where they operate. Information stored outside Canada may be subject to the laws of those countries.

7. Retention

SubDocs supports compliance and safety record-keeping, so business records (companies, documents, certificates, THA and orientation records, ratings, notes, email history and audit logs) are generally kept for as long as they are needed for AMICO’s project, safety, contractual and legal purposes. Deleted companies and documents first go to a recycle bin where they can be restored. Some temporary upload data is removed automatically within days. We have not set a single fixed retention period for all records; when information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it, subject to backup cycles and our service providers’ own retention.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, role-based access, business-unit separation and audit logging. On iOS, saved sign-in information is kept in the device’s secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect the information entrusted to us.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or correct your personal information, ask questions about how it is handled, or ask that your account be closed. Staff should contact their AMICO administrator. Subcontractors should contact their AMICO project team or use the contact details below. Closing an account stops access, but records that AMICO must keep for project, safety or legal reasons may be retained as described above. You may also have the right to complain to a privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. Children

SubDocs is a workplace tool intended for adults. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes
